Privacy Policy

Internship Copilot (iOS app, bundle com.rohitgrover.internshipcopilot), published by Rohit Grover ("we", "us").
Last updated: 2026-11-03

Summary

Internship Copilot helps you tailor resumes and cover letters to internship job descriptions. We do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Information you enter

Resume content, job descriptions you paste, cover-letter drafts, and preferences you set in the app. Content you enter is processed to generate tailored documents on or for your device session. We do not use your resume content to train public models for others.

Purchases (Pro)

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. We receive only the subscription status needed to unlock Pro features (via Apple and our subscription provider).

Diagnostics

If you opt in to share analytics with app developers in iOS, Apple may provide us anonymised crash and usage reports. These are not linked to your identity.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising. We may disclose information only if required by law.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for users seeking internships and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Retention and deletion

Deleting content in the app removes it from the app. Deleting the app removes local data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's DPDP Act, the GDPR, or Qatar's PDPPL) you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us to exercise these rights where we hold data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change and material changes will be noted in the app.
