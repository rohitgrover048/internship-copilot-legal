Privacy Policy

Internship Copilot (iOS app, bundle com.rohitgrover.internshipcopilot), published by Rohit Grover ("we", "us").
Last updated: 2026-11-03

Summary

Internship Copilot lets you paste a resume and a job description, draft a tailored resume or cover letter on your device, and open posting links in Safari. We do not store your resumes on our servers, we do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising trackers.

Information you enter

Resume text and job descriptions you paste, tailor drafts the app creates, and preferences you set in the app. That content stays on your device. We do not operate servers that store your resumes, job descriptions, or drafts, and we do not use that content to train models.

On-device drafts

Tailor drafts are generated on your device from the resume and job description you provide. Draft text is not uploaded to us.

Posting links (Safari)

When you open a job posting, the app hands an https link to Safari. Links that are not https are not opened. We do not scrape job sites, and the app does not apply to jobs for you. What you do after Safari opens is between you and that website.

Local search quota

A search quota is stored on your device so the app can limit how often you search. It is not sent to us.

Purchases (Pro)

Pro is an optional subscription purchased through Apple via the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. The app uses RevenueCat only to read whether your Apple subscription is active so Pro features can unlock. RevenueCat does not receive your resume, job description, or drafts.

Diagnostics

If you opt in to share analytics with app developers in iOS, Apple may provide us anonymised crash and usage reports. These are not linked to your identity. We do not add third-party advertising or analytics trackers of our own.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising. We may disclose information only if required by law.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for people seeking internships and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Retention and deletion

Deleting content in the app removes it from your device. Deleting the app removes local data, including pasted resumes, job descriptions, drafts, and the local search quota.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's DPDP Act, the GDPR, or Qatar's PDPPL) you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Because your resume and drafts are held on your device, you can exercise most of these directly; otherwise contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change and material changes will be noted in the app.
